Logo files in this folder
========================

Microsoft marks (microsoft-azure.svg, microsoft-icon.svg, microsoft-onedrive.svg,
microsoft-power-bi.svg, microsoft-teams.svg, microsoft-windows-icon.svg)
  Source: the "SVG Logos" collection by Gil Barbara, as distributed in the Iconify
  "logos" icon set. The collection is released under CC0 1.0 (public domain dedication).
  microsoft-windows-icon.svg is the four-pane part of the Windows logo from that set,
  cropped to a square.

Tool marks (bitdefender.svg, bitwarden.svg, paloaltonetworks.svg, ubiquiti.svg)
  Source: Simple Icons (https://simpleicons.org), released under CC0 1.0.

To remove a logo: delete its file, then in microsoft.js either replace the item's `logo`
field with a `glyph` from icons.js (STACK) or delete the `logo` field (TOOLBOX).
